How It Works

Getting started doesn't have to be complicated. Your first visit is a trial session with a trainer: we fit the suit, set the intensity for you, and you leave knowing exactly what EMS feels like. From there, you choose the rhythm.

Your questions.
Answered.

Not sure what to expect? These answers might help you feel more confident before your first session.

EMS stands for electro-muscle stimulation. You wear a suit with electrodes that send gentle impulses to your muscles while you perform simple movements with a trainer. It activates far more muscle fibres than conventional training in the same time, which is why a session is only twenty minutes.

No. It feels like a strong tingling or a deep muscle contraction. Your trainer sets the intensity for each muscle group individually and adjusts it in real time, so it is always within your comfort zone.

Not at all. EMS is low-impact and easy on the joints, which makes it a good fit for beginners, people returning from a break, and anyone short on time. We start gently and build up over the first few sessions.

Nothing. We provide the training suit and the underlayer that goes beneath it, plus towels. Just arrive about ten minutes before your session, well hydrated.

Once or twice a week, with at least 48 hours between sessions so your muscles can recover. That is where most people see steady progress without feeling drained.

EMS is safe for most healthy adults. It is not suitable during pregnancy, with a pacemaker or other implanted electronic device, or with certain medical conditions. We go through a short health questionnaire before your first session, and if in doubt we will ask you to check with your doctor.
